# Project brief

## Business problem

The programme needed to turn one source video into distinct, branded versions for multiple publishing platforms. It needed scheduling, publishing connectors, a review path, and reporting that could distinguish real distribution from activity inside an app.

The risk was scaling volume before proving that the content and measurement model were sound.

## Goal

Build a measured publishing programme that could validate the strategy cheaply, automate reliable paths, and keep unsupported or uncertain channels visibly manual.

## Scope

- Source-video and transcript handoff
- Platform-specific content generation
- Scheduling and publishing across roughly twelve Web2 platforms
- API, RSS, and manual-queue paths
- Retry, deduplication, and render-integrity controls
- Live URL checks and reporting
- Search and AI-readability audit

## Constraints

- Platform accounts, client names, credentials, source content, and live URLs remain private.
- A created post is not automatically a published or reachable post.
- Clicks, backlinks, search visibility, and AI citations require separate evidence.
- Unsupported platform capabilities must remain explicit manual work.

## Definition of done

- The publishing paths are clear for each platform.
- Failed, duplicate, truncated, and pending work is visible.
- The report separates production facts from reach and authority claims.
- The programme can be evaluated before a larger client rollout.
